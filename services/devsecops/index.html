DevSecOps and infrastructure security that teams can operate

DevSecOps makes security part of normal engineering work. Instead of adding a final approval gate, risks are identified across source, dependencies, artifacts, infrastructure, identity, network and runtime. Controls are then placed where they give useful feedback without making delivery impossible.

Delivery process

From discovery to stable operations.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    Review identity, access, secrets, network paths, artifacts, cluster policy and operational exposure.

  2. 02

    Prioritize

    Rank findings by exploitability, production impact and the practical cost of remediation.

  3. 03

    Integrate

    Add secure defaults, policy and focused checks to code, CI/CD, IaC, Kubernetes and cloud operations.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Test controls, document exceptions, monitor drift and make remediation part of normal platform ownership.

FAQ

Common engagement questions

01

Is this a penetration-testing service?

No. The focus is DevSecOps and infrastructure security: secure delivery, access, secrets, policy, cloud and Kubernetes operating controls. Specialist penetration testing can complement this work.

02

Can DevSecOps be introduced without blocking releases?

Yes. Controls are prioritized and introduced at the stage where feedback is most useful. High-noise checks and unclear ownership are treated as engineering problems.

03

Does the work include remediation?

Yes. It can include the assessment, a prioritized remediation plan, hands-on changes and integration with the team's existing delivery process.
