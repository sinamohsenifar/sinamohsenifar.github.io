FAQ

Common engagement questions

01

Do you work with Iranian cloud providers?

Yes. Experience includes ArvanCloud and Sotoon alongside AWS, Hetzner and on-prem infrastructure, including hybrid operating models.

02

Is Kubernetes always the recommended answer?

No. Kubernetes is appropriate when workload scale, deployment patterns and team operations justify its complexity. Simpler infrastructure is preferable when it meets the requirement.

03

Can cloud cost be reduced without hurting reliability?

Usually, but changes must follow workload and capacity evidence. Waste, oversized resources and inefficient architecture are addressed while preserving performance and recovery targets.
