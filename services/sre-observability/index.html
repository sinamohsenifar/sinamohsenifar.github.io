FAQ

Common engagement questions

01

Can you improve an existing Prometheus and Grafana setup?

Yes. Existing metrics, dashboards, alerts, labels, retention and ownership are reviewed first. The goal is better decisions, not simply more telemetry.

02

Does SRE work include cloud cost optimization?

Yes. Reliability and cost share the same capacity evidence. Optimization focuses on waste and architecture while protecting agreed service objectives.

03

Can failover and incident processes be reviewed separately?

Yes. A focused engagement can test failure modes, improve failover, clarify incident roles and produce practical runbooks without a larger platform project.
